# Satchel

## Unreleased

- Swapping two items now swaps their categories too. Drop an item from Bags onto one in Tanking and the new one joins Tanking, while the old one goes back to Bags. Turn it off in the gear menu.

## v0.3.2

- Talking to a banker opens your bags too, and closes them again when you leave if they weren't already open.

## v0.3.1

- **Your bank, anywhere.** Satchel saves a copy of your bank each time you visit a banker. Open it from the bag button next to Sort, or `/satchel bank`, to see and search your bank from anywhere.

## v0.3.0

- **Bank tabs.** The bank window has a row of bank tabs. Drop a bag on a tab to add its slots, drag it off to take it out, and buy the next tab from the plus.
- **Masque support.** Skin the Backpack and Bank groups from Masque's options.

## v0.2.1

- **Quest Items section.** Quest items gather in their own section at the bottom of your bags. Turn it off in the gear menu or with `/satchel quest`.
- Fixed a blue glow on every slot after a reload.
- New items glow until you hover them.
- Satchel no longer saves performance logs. `/satchel perf` shows live numbers only.

## v0.2.0

- One window for your backpack and bags, with search, sort, free slots and gold.
- A bank window.
- Your own categories, like "Tanking": Alt+right-click an item or drag it onto a header. Move sections up and down from their header's right-click menu.
- Pin items to a slot so they stay put after sorting.
- Optional reagent bag and keyring sections.
